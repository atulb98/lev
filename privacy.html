Lev privacy policy

Effective 17 October 2026.

Who uses Lev

Lev is a personal assistant used only by its owner. No one else signs in to it.

What Google data Lev accesses

With the owner's consent, Lev accesses the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive through Google's APIs.

How the data is used

Only to provide the owner's own assistant features: reading and sorting the owner's email, preparing drafts that the owner reviews and sends, reading and updating the owner's calendar, and filing documents in the owner's Drive.

Storage and sharing

Data and access tokens are kept on a private server controlled by the owner. They are not sold, rented or shared, and are not used for advertising. To carry out the owner's requests, content is processed by an AI model provider (Anthropic).

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Lev's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
